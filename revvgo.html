01 · Site redesign, 2022

A clearer look for rising professionals

As Content Marketing Specialist, one of my first tasks was to redesign RevvGo’s site and navigation to improve the experience and freshen the look. The style spoke to its audience: rising professionals and young millennials. I handled branding, design, copy and navigation in HubSpot, Canva and Copy.ai.

02 · B2B rebuild

A new site in one month

When RevvGo shifted its focus to business clients, the site needed a more elevated redesign. I designed the whole site and owned the UX: navigation, color palette, typography, imagery and content, and rebuilt it on Wix in one month, moving all CRM data and workflows over from HubSpot.

The new homepage tells clients more about the company, with stats to encourage sign-ups. Growth guides now bundle curated articles, videos and podcasts, and work on desktop, tablet and phone.

03 · Graphics & campaigns

Content for every channel

I also made the imagery for RevvGo’s website, emails, social media and ads, including a 3-month paid campaign on Meta, LinkedIn and Google that reached 12,000+ users. Tools: Canva, HubSpot, Google Ads, Meta Ads and LinkedIn Ads.
