Featured · product design

United MileagePlus X

I led the redesign of Dfinitiv's app into a white-labeled iOS and Android app for United Airlines. I worked with United's UX, brand, marketing, legal and engineering teams to meet their guidelines and get approvals, then turned their requests into clear direction for our engineers.

It launched in November 2025, 90 days after the contract was signed. It now has 400,000+ users and a 4.8 rating from 21k App Store reviews. Members use it to earn MileagePlus miles on shopping, dining, eGift cards and offers. My App Store imagery and mockups appear on United's websites and on in-flight seatback screens.
