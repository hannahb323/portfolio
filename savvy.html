Product design · Dfinitiv

Savvy

A consumer app that helps people save money: pay with the right card, activate the offers they already have, and hear about deals before they check out.

iOS Launched Aug 2023 Lead designer

Role

Lead designer: UX, UI, brand and design system

Team

A 10–15 person startup: engineering, QA, business and leadership

Launch

“Savvy – Spend Smart” on the App Store, August 31, 2023

Tools

Figma

The problem

Most people carry several cards and hundreds of card-linked offers, and still leave money on the table at checkout.

They pay with whichever card is on top, forget to activate offers, and find out about deals after they’ve already bought. Savvy puts the right card and the right offer in front of them at the moment they’re about to spend.

The App Store screenshots above show the app as it launched. Most screens below are design concepts I explored alongside it. Merchant names, cards and numbers are sample data.

01 · Home

The best way to pay, at a glance

The home screen answers “which card should I use?” before you ask. Every spending category maps to the best card in your wallet, followed by featured offers, a watchlist, what’s expiring and shopping guides.

02 · Wallet

Every card, and what it’s worth

Connecting cards kicks off offer discovery, so new users see value right away. The wallet then shows each card and loyalty program with its live offers and their total value.

03 · Recommendations

The right next card

Savvy looks at your spending and recommends cards that fit it, with the estimated savings for each of your top categories, so the case for switching is concrete.

04 · Merchants

Know before you check out

Each store page ranks the cards in your wallet by how much you’d save there and lists every offer, with the details and the card to use. A full-screen view makes a single offer feel like something to shop.

05 · Brand storefronts

Everything about a brand, in one place

Every brand gets its own branded storefront: a look at the space and products, its top offers, nearby locations, the guides it appears in, and a chat to ask about it. Brands can also promote a message at the top. These two show a local coffee roaster and a hotel group, with rewards earned in miles through an airline partner.

06 · Savvy AI

Ask instead of searching

A chat assistant answers questions like “what cards should I have in my wallet?” using your spending, and shows the cards it recommends. A clear reminder asks people to double-check AI advice before they act on it.

What happened next

Savvy launched on the App Store on August 31, 2023 and built a following on social media. Dfinitiv then pivoted to B2B, and the company now builds SmartCXP, a platform that brings brands’ loyalty programs into AI assistants.
